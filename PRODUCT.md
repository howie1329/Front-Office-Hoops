# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the developer/operator tuning Front Office Hoops' simulation systems. They use developer labs to inspect generated data, adjust bounded configuration values, reproduce seeded runs, and compare model behavior before it becomes gameplay.

## Product Purpose

Front Office Hoops is a browser-hosted basketball front-office simulation. The V2 Developer Labs area provides a direct, inspectable workspace for calibrating simulation models without requiring a complete playable league. The Player Generation lab should make it easy to change generation settings, generate repeatable players or populations, inspect diagnostics, and export results for analysis.

## Brand Personality

Precise, trustworthy, experimental.

## Anti-references

Avoid sports-media presentation, fantasy-sports betting dashboards, game-store decoration, promotional SaaS layouts, and opaque black-box controls. The lab should not feel like a player-card collector or hide model assumptions behind polished visuals.

## Design Principles

- Make every generated result reproducible from visible inputs.
- Put model controls next to the evidence they affect.
- Show enough internal diagnostics to explain surprising outcomes.
- Prefer dense, readable data surfaces over decorative presentation.
- Keep experimental tools isolated from normal league gameplay.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional accessibility compliance target is required for this developer-only surface beyond preserving the existing component behavior and avoiding regressions in keyboard focus, labels, and readable contrast.
